## 🎼 Project Overview & Suggested Listening Framework

The `AMAP-Sound-OS-Kernel` is an open-source artistic and educational music framework composed and produced by **Strategist Kongming Huang Yueying**. It combines commercially distributed audio works with open-access score notation files in raw ABC format.

The project explores the mathematical philosophy of **$(-3) \times (-3) = 9$**—a structural framework illustrating how adversity and challenges ($-3$), when met with deliberate, strategic internal alignment ($-3$), can be converted into exponential growth, resilience, and personal sovereignty ($+9$).

---

## 🔄 3-Step Optional Listening & Reflection Protocol

To engage deeply with the structural themes of these compositions, listeners are invited to try the following voluntary 3-step practice:

1. **Active Listening**: Engage consciously with the pitch, tempo, dynamics, and waveforms, using music as an anchor for focused attention.
2. **Reflective Visualization**: Envision your past experiences, current challenges, ideal self, and future aspirations in alignment with the musical progression.
3. **Decisive Practice**: Upon the conclusion of a track, consider making a conscious, voluntary commitment toward a specific positive action or task in your daily life.
